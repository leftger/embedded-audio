ITU-T G.711 speech companding codecs (µ-law and A-law).

G.711 encodes 13-14 bit dynamic range into 8-bit log-compressed audio samples. Ubiquitous in telecommunications, IoT intercoms, walkie-talkies, and industrial voice prompts.
